Privacy Policy

Built Here: Car Loan Deduction (iPhone app) · Effective: November 22, 2026

Short version: everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. To decode a VIN, it sends only the VIN to NHTSA's public VIN service.

What stays on your device

Your vehicles, VINs, eligibility answers, loan details, interest amounts, filing status and income (MAGI) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local database. They are not uploaded to us or to any server we run. We don't run any servers for this app.

What leaves your device

  • VIN lookups (NHTSA). When you decode a VIN, the app sends the VIN, and only the VIN, to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's public vPIC service (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov) over HTTPS. No name, location, device ID or other information is added. NHTSA's own privacy policy applies to that request. Each VIN is looked up once and the answer is cached on your iPhone.
  • Purchases (Apple). The optional Tax Pack is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. The app only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase exists, and we never see your payment details.
  • Files you share. If you share the PDF summary, it goes only where you send it (for example Mail, Messages or Files).

Camera

The camera is used only to scan the VIN, on the device. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Tax-time reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push service is used.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't receive any.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. Both remove everything the app stored. Your Tax Pack purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored.

Children

The app is meant for adults who finance a vehicle. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
