Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Bead Pattern Maker processes your designs on your device.

Charts and projects

Biser processes the photos, PDFs and bead charts you choose on your iPhone or iPad. It saves your projects, palettes, materials, language preference and current weaving row on that device. You can use the editor without an account. The app includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Purchases

Apple handles payment and purchase restoration. The app asks StoreKit whether your Apple Account has access to Pro. The publisher does not receive your payment card details through the app.

Files you choose to share

When you export a pattern, print it or save an editable project to Files, you select the destination. A recipient or a file provider may keep its own copy. Your device backups and any storage provider you use follow your Apple Account and device settings.

Deleting a project

In My projects, touch and hold a project, choose Delete project and confirm. Removing the app deletes its local library. Copies you exported or backed up can be removed separately from their respective locations.

This website and support

GitHub Pages serves this website. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. The pages contain no advertising scripts, tracking pixels or contact forms. If you email support, the publisher and email provider receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. The publisher uses them to respond and handle follow-up. Contact ikorkod@gmail.com with privacy questions or to request deletion of a support conversation. GitHub Privacy Statement.
