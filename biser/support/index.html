Help with Biser

Import a chart, follow its rows and keep an editable copy of your work.

Open your first chart

Tap Try an example in My projects to open the Heart pattern. Check its three materials and tap Create and start weaving. If there are already projects in your library, use Create project to start another.

Import a PDF or photo

Choose a file on your device. Select the chart area, align the grid and compare the colors and counts with the original. You can correct individual beads or trace a design manually when a symbol or color is hard to recognize.

Return to your row

Tap Continue on the project card. In Weaving, follow the bead order, advance a row or change the reading direction. The Materials screen lists the beads required for each color.

Keep an editable backup

In My projects, touch and hold a project and choose Save project to Files. To restore it, use Open from Files and select the .beadproject file. Export a PDF when you want a printed chart.

Restore access to Pro

Use the Apple Account you used for the purchase, connect to the internet and choose Restore purchase on the Pro screen in My projects. If the store is temporarily unavailable, try again later.

Contact the developer

Email ikorkod@gmail.com. Include your app version, your iPhone or iPad model and the step where you need help. You can describe the issue without sending your pattern.
